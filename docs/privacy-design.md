# Privacy design baseline

This document records engineering intent. It is not a legal privacy policy or a guarantee about a future commercial service.

- Minimise collection and processing to data genuinely required for the user's purchasing goal.
- Require no user accounts for the competition baseline.
- Do not plan to collect or store payment data in CoSource; payments remain with merchants.
- Avoid unnecessary analytics, tracking, and cookies.
- Prefer session-local state until durable persistence has a demonstrated requirement.
- Define explicit retention, deletion, and export policies before adding commercial persistence.
- Complete privacy and jurisdiction-specific legal review before commercial operation.

Implementation details, vendor assessments, user-rights workflows, and formal notices are deferred until their associated features and jurisdictions are known.
