# Canonical supplier intelligence

Supplier intelligence is durable, project-scoped state owned by the CoSource application kernel. A canonical supplier has a stable CoSource identity and a human-controlled standing (`preferred`, `allowed`, or `excluded`). Display names never establish identity. External merchants are linked only by an explicit human operation using their exact source-scoped identity, and one such identity can belong to only one supplier in a project.

Each supplier retains bounded histories: at most 20 received quotations, 50 contact events, 50 reliability observations, and 20 risks; a project holds at most 50 suppliers. Capacity is checked before mutation. Quotations are bound to an exact requirement identity and revision plus a described resource or exact candidate/offer subject. They reuse canonical cost components, coverage, evidence strength, and integer-minor-unit money. Active quotations are selected by represented observation instant and then stable identity—not array position. Withdrawn, superseded, and expired quotations remain history but cannot establish current readiness.

Reliability is a qualitative derivation from retained observations. Missing history is unknown, conflicting current observations remain conflicting, and expired observations are stale. Risks are explicit evidence, never inferred from a name or absence of data. Resolution is a human operation. Excluded standing and decision-grade active critical risks block readiness. Verification-only critical-risk evidence requires verification and cannot produce readiness.

Candidate and purchase-plan evaluation resolve supplier information only through exact merchant links. `evaluatePurchasePlan` remains the sole plan evaluator, and canonical costing remains the sole landed-cost engine. Supplier evidence never mutates a plan, sends a message, requests a quote, places an order, or authorizes payment.

The version-one workspace envelope accepts older payloads with no `suppliers` member and restores them as an empty supplier collection. New saves include supplier state in durable fingerprinting and retain existing atomic restoration, CAS, recovery, and saved-project isolation guarantees.

Evidence strength is owned by the neutral evidence domain. `source_explicit` and `cosource_derived` are decision-grade; `source_inferred` and `unknown` require verification. Readiness is conservatively composed in the order known failure, known conflict, insufficient evidence, verification required, then ready.

Future-dated observations never apply. Validity endpoints are inclusive. A risk review date is a review-due marker, not expiry: an unresolved risk stays active until explicit resolution, and overdue review remains visible. Quotations distinguish missing, other-revision, future, expired, historical, current-partial, current-verification, and current-complete states.

The kernel—not callers—assigns quotation identity, supplier binding, active requirement identity/revision, observation time, and initial status. Candidate and offer subjects are checked against retained evidence and the supplier's exact merchant link before mutation. Supplier mutations and their bounded activity record publish coherently.

Reliability precedence is conservative and order-independent: known decision-grade negatives and conflicts remain blocking; otherwise any applicable verification-only or unknown observation prevents a positive or ready conclusion until verified. Quotation operations validate runtime structure before reading subject fields and return bounded failures without mutation. Presentation drafts are keyed by workspace plus supplier identity. Future-dated active-status risks remain visible history, are excluded from current readiness, are labelled future-dated rather than active or overdue, and expose no premature resolution action.

Supplier-risk time is classified once by the provider-neutral domain as `future`, `active`, `review_overdue`, or `resolved`, independently of any purchase requirement and retained-array order. Observation and review timestamps equal to the current instant are current and not overdue. The application kernel exposes that canonical result through the read-only `getSupplierRiskStatus` operation using the application's clock. It works without an active goal, returns bounded failures for unknown identities, and does not mutate workspace state, notify subscribers, or alter a purchase plan. Supplier readiness and the React presentation use the same classifier. Current and review-overdue risks remain explicitly resolvable with or without an active goal; future and resolved history remain non-actionable. Requirement-bound quotation assessment deliberately continues to require an active goal. This application-local read boundary does not expand the 13-tool WebMCP surface.
