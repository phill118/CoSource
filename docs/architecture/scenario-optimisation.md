# Canonical scenario and optimisation engine

R9 derives bounded purchase-plan alternatives from the active project’s canonical Resource Requirement, retained product observations, human verification, landed-cost evidence, and exactly linked supplier intelligence. The provider-neutral scenario domain does not search, refresh, communicate, purchase, or persist derived alternatives. The application kernel remains the sole live authority and the existing `evaluatePurchasePlan` chain remains the sole evaluator.

Each read computes an immutable basis from workspace identity, requirement identity and revision, purchase-plan identity, revision and lines, market, retained observation identities/sequences/timestamps, human-verification identities and revisions, and supplier assessment inputs. A deterministic fingerprint binds every scenario to that basis. Evidence refresh, verification, supplier evidence, requirement edits, selected offers, quantities, or plan revision therefore changes the basis and makes an older scenario ID inapplicable.

Generation has two explicit modes. An empty plan considers at most 25 retained candidates as alternative starting plans, but never defaults an absent goal quantity to one. An existing plan resolves every referenced product from the full bounded evidence ledger and supports all 50 canonical plan lines; unrelated retained evidence cannot displace a plan subject. It preserves every product, line identity, quantity, and unit semantic while enumerating exact offer combinations. Both modes evaluate at most 100 combinations and present at most five non-duplicated results. Candidate/combination truncation is reported separately from the five-result presentation limit.

There is no weighted score or universal winner. Mandatory readiness precedes preferences. Objective labels and dominance are calculated across the complete evaluated bounded draft set before result limiting. Non-dominated leaders are retained first; returned dominance references never point outside the returned set. Merchant count participates only when every merchant identity is exact. Supplier risk compares decision-grade cumulative severity thresholds qualitatively: critical-or-higher, high-or-higher, medium-or-higher, and low-or-higher. One profile leads only when it is no worse at every threshold, better at least once, and dominates every other supported comparable profile. A profile requires at least one applicable decision-grade active risk; absence of recorded risk is unsupported and is never interpreted as proof of zero risk. Standing and general readiness are not risk values; incomplete risk evidence and severity trade-offs are incomparable. Mixed currencies, incomplete bounds, and unsupported delivery or quality evidence remain incomparable.

Only alternatives whose canonical plan evaluation is `ready_on_current_evidence` and has no blocking or verification issue can be applied. Application accepts a strict scenario-ID and basis-fingerprint command, recomputes the current set, and replaces the whole plan through the existing revision boundary once. Existing line IDs survive offer-choice scenarios; a new empty-plan line receives an application-owned ID only after validation. Stale, blocked, conditional, unknown, or malformed requests consume no ID and preserve all state.

One application-owned instant is captured per complete scenario-set read. The basis records distinct qualitative phases for observation time and freshness expiry; human-verification start and inclusive expiry; quotation observation, inclusive `validFrom`, and inclusive expiry; reliability observation and inclusive expiry; and supplier-risk observation and review-overdue transition. Start boundaries become applicable at the boundary, expiry remains applicable through the boundary, and review becomes overdue only after its boundary. Raw moving milliseconds are excluded, so repeated reads within one phase are identical; crossing a material boundary changes the fingerprint and rejects the old scenario.

React presents ready, conditional, and blocked alternatives without calling conditional plans achievable. The existing `evaluate_purchase_plan` WebMCP read exposes at most five bounded summaries and their basis, while the registered tool count remains thirteen and no scenario mutation tool exists. S7 operational work—supplier communication, enquiries, purchase orders, checkout, payment, fulfilment, completion, and reconciliation—is outside this boundary.
